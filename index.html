Host Permissions & Rules

As the host, you can give or deny permission for certain actions. For example, if strikes are set to "NO" in the config, a player cannot just start a strike unless you specifically give that individual player permission to do so. You can play normally as a host, but you hold higher administrative owner permissions.

Recommended Settings & SCP Spawning

We recommend checking everything (turning everything ON) if you want to play a normal, more interesting SCP roleplay experience with active SCPs. However, if you are worried about lag or do not want SCP monsters, you don't have to spawn anything.

The host receives an Ender Chest containing labeled SCP spawn eggs. If SCPs are enabled, either you or your friends must manually spawn each SCP in their respective locations.

Performance & Management Tips

To reduce lag and save memory, we recommend using commands like looking at an SCP with your crosshair and typing kill, or using target selectors / kill commands on specific entities when an SCP is not needed. When a test is about to happen, you can simply respawn them. If you just want to have fun without monsters, turn off SCPs, enable strikes, and disable things like rank changes!
